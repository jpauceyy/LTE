From the trading floor to trading independently.

How nearly 10 years of independent trading began, and why it shapes the way Learn Try Earn teaches.

  1. The ambition

    A seat on a London trading floor.

    My interest in financial markets originally led me to consider a career in investment banking in London, with the ambition of working on a trading floor. I was drawn to the analytical thinking, fast-moving environment and challenge of making decisions as markets responded to events around the world.

  2. The decision

    Choosing to trade independently.

    As I considered the direction I wanted my life to take, I decided that trading independently was a better fit for me. It offered the freedom to develop my own approach and build my working life around my priorities, while taking responsibility for my own decisions.

  3. The responsibility

    Independence comes with accountability.

    You have to establish your own structure, question your judgement and accept the consequences when an idea does not work out.

  4. Today

    Learn Try Earn.

    Those principles shape the approach I want to share through Learn Try Earn: explaining the markets I trade and helping you build a clearer understanding of trading.

The principles behind the process.

Trading on your own means nobody else sets the rules. These three come first.

Establish your own structure

Decide what you trade, what you’re looking for and how much you’re prepared to risk before the market gives you a reason to act.

Question your judgement

Test every idea against the chart and the plan. Feeling confident is not a reason to enter a trade.

Accept the consequences

Some ideas won’t work out. Planning the downside first means a loss is an outcome you prepared for, not a surprise.

The goal of Learn Try Earn

A practical understanding of financial markets, with gold and silver at the centre and Bitcoin and Ethereum alongside. Built on a process you understand.

Whether you are starting from scratch or looking to bring more structure to your approach, the aim is to help you develop stronger foundations.

Understand the market

Know what moves gold, silver and crypto, and when each is most active.

Prepare a plan

Decide what you’re looking for before the session starts.

Manage risk

Define the downside first and size every position from it.

Review honestly

Look back at every decision, including the ones that didn’t work.

Experience doesn’t remove uncertainty.

Nearly 10 years of independent trading provides the background to this platform, but experience does not remove uncertainty. Losses are part of trading, and no strategy or educational service can guarantee profitability.

What I want to offer is a clear, grounded learning environment where the emphasis is on knowledge, personal responsibility and developing a process you understand.

Vantage + MetaTrader 4 or 5

How to get started, step by step.

Open an account with our broker, Vantage, verify your details and connect it to MetaTrader 4 or MetaTrader 5. Most people are set up within a working day.

Open a Vantage account Already have one? Link MetaTrader

Have these ready

An email address and phone number, a photo ID (passport, driving licence or national ID card) and a proof of address dated within the last few months, such as a utility bill or bank statement.

Open your Vantage account.

Four steps from sign-up to a funded account. Exact screens differ a little by country.

  1. Step 01

    Register with Vantage.

    Go to the Open Live Account page using our link, choose an Individual account, then enter your country, email, phone number and a password. This creates your Vantage Client Portal login.

  2. Step 02

    Verify your identity.

    Complete your profile and upload a photo ID and proof of address. This is a standard anti-money-laundering (KYC) check. Review usually takes up to one business day, and Vantage emails you when you’re approved.

  3. Step 03

    Choose MT4 or MT5.

    When you set up your trading account, pick the platform (MetaTrader 4 or MetaTrader 5), the account type and your base currency. Not sure? MT5 is the newer platform; either works for gold, silver and crypto.

  4. Step 04

    Fund your account.

    Once approved, deposit from the Client Portal. Start with an amount you can afford to lose, or open a demo account first and practise without real money.

Link it to MetaTrader 4 or 5.

MetaTrader is where you’ll see the charts and place trades. You log in with the trading account Vantage created for you.

01

Find your login details

Vantage emails your trading account number and server after setup. You can also find them in the Client Portal under Accounts, next to MT4 or MT5.

02

Install MetaTrader

Download the version that matches your account (MT4 or MT5) from the Vantage Client Portal, or get the MetaTrader app from the App Store or Google Play.

03

Log in to your account

Enter your account number and trading password, then choose the exact Vantage server shown in your portal. Type the password by hand rather than pasting it.

On desktop

Open MetaTrader, go to File → Login to Trade Account, enter your account number and password, select your Vantage server and click Login.

On mobile

In the MetaTrader app, go to Settings → New Account (or Manage Accounts → +), search for Vantage, pick your server and sign in.

Check it worked

You’ll see your balance in the Trade tab and live prices moving. If it says “Invalid account”, check the server name and that MT4/MT5 matches your account.

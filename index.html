Precious metals first. Crypto second.

Gold and silver sit at the centre of everything we teach. Bitcoin and Ethereum are traded occasionally and make up a smaller part of the activity. Here’s what every beginner should know about each.

Main focus

Where most of the analysis and trading happens

Gold, XAUUSD

Gold vs US dollar
What it is
The price of one ounce of gold in US dollars.
What moves it
Interest rate expectations, the US dollar, inflation data and global uncertainty.
Busiest
London and New York sessions, and around US data such as CPI and NFP.
Beginner tip
Gold can move fast. Size on your stop distance, not your confidence.

Silver, XAGUSD

Silver vs US dollar
What it is
The price of one ounce of silver in US dollars.
What moves it
The US dollar, interest rate expectations, industrial demand and the direction of gold.
Busiest
London and New York sessions, much like gold.
Beginner tip
Silver often moves more sharply than gold in percentage terms, so size positions carefully.
Traded occasionally

A smaller part of the activity

Bitcoin, BTCUSD

Trades around the clock and moves further in percentage terms, so a smaller size can carry the same risk.

Ethereum, ETHUSD

Often follows Bitcoin’s direction with larger swings. Driven by sentiment, risk appetite and crypto news.
